Overview

The annual Spark Conference, which is targeted at entrepreneurs and tech professionals, was supported by the Spark Event Website. It displays speakers and timetables, advertises the event, and enables user registration.

Objectives

  • Publicize the occasion
  • Make registrations possible
  • Show schedule and speakers
  • Make sure the design is responsive

Key Features

  • Speaker directory
  • Interactive schedule
  • Countdown timer
  • Social media sharing

Results

2,000+ ticket sales and over 10,000 visits. The design and navigational simplicity were commended by users. Excellent performance even in heavy traffic

Lessons Learned

acquired expertise in cross-device optimization, real-time event updates, and scalable architecture. enhanced dialogue with stakeholders and clients.
